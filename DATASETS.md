## Notes
- The own-env controls set covers only the **cheating** output. Every collusion-related output needs multi-agent data; today that is the small board-seed pilot. External multi-agent sources (ai-village) are candidates for **unseen-author evaluation** (Probe B.II), not training, unless their license permits training.
- "Derivable" labels from transcripts are heuristics (regex/graph), to be confirmed by a reader or judge; our own instrumented env is the only source that can label all six outputs at once.
