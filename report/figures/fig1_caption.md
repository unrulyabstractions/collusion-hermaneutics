**Figure 1.** We test transfer to an unseen author (type B.II) in the solo arm. In the solo arm the prompt never mentions the exploit. Each row group is one held-out author. We train each method on the other four authors and score the held-out author's agents. The line under each author's name gives its think coverage. Think coverage is the share of the author's turns whose think block holds readable reasoning. Each marker is a held-out AUROC. Its whisker is the 95% bootstrap interval over runs. The three filled markers are probes on the reader model's residual stream. The PCA probe uses all layers at the think-open and think-close tokens. The every-dimension probe uses all layers at the think-close token. The per-turn probe uses layer 38 at the think-close token. It scores each agent by the mean of its turn scores. The hollow grey marker is the text baseline. The text baseline is a TF-IDF logistic regression on the whole rendered transcript. The dashed line marks chance.
